Technical Comments

Additionally, multiple image files have been integrated throughout the website to enhance its visual appeal.

Aesthetics of the Website

The website has been designed with a clean, intuitive layout to ensure users can navigate it with ease . Its simple user interface and visually appealing colour scheme aims to enhance the overall user experience. By adopting a modern, minimalist approach, the design not only reduces clutter but also creates a more engaging and aesthetically pleasing environment for users.

Colour and Font

The specific colours used include:

  • Black: #0a0a0a
  • Brown #4d3e30
  • Off-white:#f0f0f0

Design Choices

The website uses a carefully chosen colour palette of warm brown (#4d3e30), off-white (#f0f0f0) and dark text (#0a0a0a) maintained consistently across all four pages. This limited palette creates a cohesive, elegant aesthetic that feels intentional and refined. The warm brown tones were specifically chosen to evoke a sense of sophistication and warmth, making the website feel personal and inviting rather than clinical or corporate.

The typography is built entirely around Playfair Display, a classic serif font known for its high contrast between thick and thin strokes. This gives the website a editorial, magazine-style quality that elevates the overall aesthetic. The logo uses PT Serif in italic bold, distinguishing it visually from the rest of the page and giving the name a signature-like quality. Both fonts were sourced from Google Fonts, ensuring they load reliably across all browsers.

The navigation bar is presented as a bordered box with individual dividers between each link i.e. About Me, My Past, My Future and Comments. This table-cell style design makes the navigation immediately recognisable and easy to use.

Each page, aside from the comments section, follows the same layout: logo and nav in the header, content and image side by side in the main area, and a footer at the bottom. This consistency means users always know where to find information regardless of which page they are on. The max-width of 1024px prevents the content from stretching uncomfortably wide on large screens, keeping the reading experience comfortable.

Images are placed on the right side of every page, balancing the text on the left. Each image has a brown border and rounded corners matching the overall colour scheme, so they feel like a deliberate design element rather than an afterthought. The images chosen, a painted moon, clouds and flowers, all share a warm, muted, painterly quality that complements the brown and off-white palette perfectly.

The footer is minimal and consistent, featuring only the copyright text in brown, separated from the content by a thin brown line. This keeps the bottom of every page clean and uncluttered. The custom scrollbar is also styled in brown to match the theme, ensuring even the browser UI elements feel part of the overall design.

Padding and spacing are used generously between the header and content, within cards, and around images so the page never feels crowded. The absence of heavy backgrounds or drop shadows keeps the design feeling light and airy, which suits the elegant, understated aesthetic of the site.

Interactivity

CSS enhances user interactivity throughout the website, most notably in the navigation bar. When the mouse cursor hovers over any of the four navigation links, the background of that link fills with brown (#4d3e30) and the text switches to off-white (#f0f0f0). This is achieved using the nav a:hover selector in the CSS. This immediate visual response tells the user exactly which page they are about to click on, reducing confusion and improving the overall navigation experience. The smooth transition: 0.3s property ensures the colour change happens gradually rather than abruptly, making the interaction feel polished and intentional rather than jarring.

Similarly, all hyperlinks across the website have a hover effect applied via a:hover, where the text colour shifts to brown and a subtle warm glow appears using text-shadow. This reinforces to the user that the text is clickable and interactive, without being distracting when the user is simply reading.

The website is also designed to remain visually consistent across different browsers such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Firefox. The use of widely supported Google Fonts, standard CSS flexbox for layout, and a fixed max-width of 1024px ensures that the content, images and navigation do not shift or break depending on the browser being used. The box-sizing: border-box rule applied to all elements via the universal * selector further prevents unexpected layout changes by ensuring padding and borders are calculated within the element's set dimensions rather than outside them. This gives users across all devices and browsers the same clean, readable and consistent experience regardless of how they access the website.

Accessibility

To ensure accessibility, this website has been designed to cater to users with a range of different needs and disabilities.

Alt text has been provided for all images used across the four pages. For example, the painted moon image on the About Me page and the floral painting on the My Past page both include descriptive alt attributes. This is important for users who rely on screen readers as they cannot see the images directly, as well as for situations where images fail to load due to low bandwidth or connection issues.

The primary font used throughout the website is Playfair Display, a serif font chosen for its high legibility and clear distinction between characters. While serif fonts can sometimes be harder to read at small sizes, Playfair Display is specifically designed with strong contrast between strokes, making it comfortable to read at the font sizes used across the site. Paragraph text is set at 1rem (16px) and headings are scaled larger — h1 at 2.5rem and h2 proportionally smaller — so that users with visual or cognitive impairments can clearly distinguish between the page title, subheadings and body text.

The colour scheme of warm brown (#4d3e30) text and headings against an off-white (#f0f0f0) background provides strong contrast without being harsh on the eyes. Extremely bright or saturated colours have been deliberately avoided, as these can cause discomfort for users with visual sensitivities or conditions such as photophobia. The warm, muted tones are gentle and easy to read for extended periods.

The navigation bar uses a clearly bordered box with distinct dividers between each link, and hover effects that change the background colour to brown when a link is highlighted. This makes it easy for users with motor impairments to identify which link their cursor is positioned over before clicking.

No audio or auto-playing media has been included anywhere on the website, ensuring the experience is fully accessible for users with hearing or auditory impairments without requiring any additional adjustments.
